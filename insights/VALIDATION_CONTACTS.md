# Validation contacts — researchers worth pre-validating with

People to consider sending a 1-page summary to once a finding is firm enough
to validate against expert opinion. From parallel-Claude review.

The pattern: send a focused 1-page summary with the specific question, not
"please review my work". Hit rate goes way up if the question is sharp and
the artefact is small.

## How to actually use this

This list is just a directory. Do NOT mail anyone unless we have a result
firm enough to discuss with a real expert. The criterion for "firm enough":

- A finding documented in a diary entry with confidence ≥ medium-high.
- A 1-page summary you'd be willing to print and hand to the contact in
  person.
- A specific question, not "what do you think?".

Once those three are met, the contact list shortens decision-friction.
