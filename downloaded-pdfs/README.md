## Source / licensing notes

- All Springer volumes obtained from Bruce Berndt directly for personal research use.
- The 1927 Hardy-Aiyar-Wilson collected papers are public domain (PD-old) and have been on archive.org for years; this is just a clean Kerala-PL scan.
- The RIMS 2002 Berndt-Yee paper is on the RIMS open archive.
- Private repo so on-disk inclusion is fine for our purposes; do **not** redistribute outside this repo.

## What's still missing

Worth asking Berndt directly. Note: `hal-03912911.pdf` is the 2022 HRJ *reproduction* of the QRs themselves, NOT the 1984 commentary paper.
- **Berndt *Notebooks Parts II, III & IV*** (Springer 1989, 1991, 1994) — middle chapters (theta functions, modular equations at low primes). Complements the Part 1 + Part V bracket we now have. Lower priority than the unblocked Hunts.

## Hunt-unblock summary after the May 24 additions

The full Berndt-family commentary corpus we now have:
- Notebooks Part 1 (Ch. 1-9) and Part V (Ch. 30-37) — front + back of the 5-volume series; missing the middle.
- Lost Notebook Parts I-IV (the complete Andrews-Berndt series).
- Plus two focused secondary sources (Berndt-Yee RIMS 2002 on Eisenstein series; Berndt-Dixit arXiv 2009 on the αβ=1 Mellin-pair transformation).

This is enough to:
- Calibrate Hunt 5 against Berndt's editorial judgment (NB Part 1 Ch. 3-4 vs QR I + QR II material).
- Resolve Hunt 1's H1a pagination ambiguity (LN Part IV ch. on 1729).
- Execute Hunts 2, 3, 4 with full commentary (NB Part V).
- Open the W7 fractional bridge with a concrete Mellin-pair-with-αβ=1 example to start from (Berndt-Dixit 2009).
