# Rights-limited / not included

This pack intentionally does **not** redistribute full copyrighted AMS/Springer/Cambridge books from unofficial mirrors, even when search engines expose PDFs. In particular, I did not include:

- Bruce C. Berndt and Robert A. Rankin, *Ramanujan: Letters and Commentary* (AMS History of Mathematics 9, 1995). The Quarterly Reports content Claude wanted is covered here by the open Hardy-Ramanujan Journal reproduction plus extracted text.
- George E. Andrews and Bruce C. Berndt, *Ramanujan's Lost Notebook, Part IV* (Springer, 2013). Instead, this pack includes the original lost-notebook facsimile PDF/pages and open-access 1729/K3 context.
- Bruce C. Berndt, *Ramanujan's Notebooks*, Parts III-V, including Part V Chapter 32. Instead, this pack includes Bhatnagar's open-access q-continued-fractions roadmap and the original notebook facsimile PDFs.
- Shaun Cooper, *Ramanujan's Theta Functions* (Springer, 2017).

For all of these, see `source_status/source_status.csv` and `source_status/blocked_or_rights_limited_sources.csv` for bibliographic targets and suggested substitutes.
